FAQ

How do I restore “Remove Ads”?

Open the in-game shop and tap “Restore Purchases” while signed in to the same Google Play account or Apple ID you bought it with.

I didn’t receive my coins

Restart the game and check your internet connection. If it persists, email us with the order number from your store receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play or Apple under their policies.

How do I change personalized ads?

From the game Settings → Privacy, or from your device settings.
